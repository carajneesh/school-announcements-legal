School Announcements

A small personal tool that reads school notices published on a school portal and turns the dated items into Google Calendar events. It also sends a short daily digest by email to its owner.

It is built and used by one family. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

What it does with Google

  • Creates and updates calendars that the app itself creates, and adds events to them (Google Calendar scope calendar.app.created).
  • Creates its own school task lists and adds and updates tasks in them (Google Tasks scope tasks).
  • Sends the digest email from the owner's own account (Gmail scope gmail.send). It cannot read, search or delete any email.
