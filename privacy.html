Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

School Announcements ("the app") is a personal tool used by a single family. This page explains what Google data it touches.

What the app accesses

  • Google Calendar: only calendars that the app creates itself. It adds and updates events in them. It cannot see or change any other calendar.
  • Gmail: send-only access, used to send one digest email to the account owner. The app cannot read, list, search or delete any email.

The app does not access Google Drive, Contacts or any other Google service.

What the app stores

The app runs on the owner's own computer. It keeps a local copy of school notices and the identifiers of the calendars it created. It does not copy or store the contents of your Gmail or of any calendar other than its own.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party. School notices (not Google data) are read by an AI assistant running under the owner's own account to work out dates and actions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Revoking access and deleting data

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local folder removes the stored notices and calendar identifiers.
